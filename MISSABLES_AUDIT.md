# Missables audit

Last updated: 2026-10-25

The registry is a partial research artifact. It contains 138 profile-scoped records across all four discs, including Summit Station stock targets, Treno Air Racket recovery, Chain Mail stock, Memoria's returnable pickups and side activities, Ozma, Hammer's ending-scene choice, final-battle cutoff and steals, and Nero Family checkpoints. All 138 warnings now link to one of 97 route cutoff windows, including every high/critical record (79); the data audit enforces complete warning coverage. Multi-step activities use separate stable objectives for distinct stages when accepting an item and delivering it have different cutoffs; Mois's Ice Cavern letter pickup and Dali delivery are tracked separately. Area-clearance rows track a lost pickup location; they do not claim every contained item is impossible to obtain elsewhere. The full inventory of shop stock, individual field items, steals, encounters, Blue Magic, later quest steps, and platform achievements are still outstanding.

## App behavior

Missable records point to stable objective IDs and warning chapters. The overview uses the selected profile goal, route, current story area, and marks to show the next open cutoff or an unmarked past cutoff. If no current area is selected, or a warning has no mapped chapter, its timing is shown as unplaced instead of being called an open window. Passing a warning chapter only means the cutoff needs review; it does not prove permanent loss. Chapter rows show current/future warning counts. Progress remains local to each playthrough profile.

## Required classifications

- Permanently missable item or outcome.
- Missable opportunity with another acquisition path.
- Recoverable later, with next availability, difficulty, rarity, prerequisites, and costs.
- Limited quantity.
- Strongly recommended now because the later path is substantially harder.
- Mutually exclusive outcome.
- Time-sensitive event or timer.
- Conditional opportunity.
- Version-dependent behavior.

## Audit inventory: major missable-content classes

The classes below define the research scope; they are **not** claims that every record has been found or verified. For each candidate, investigate the cutoff trigger, last safe action, quantity, alternative path, relative cost/difficulty, affected platform/build, selected completion profiles, and source evidence before enabling a warning.

| Class to investigate | Candidate content and verification question |
| --- | --- |
| Treasure and field pickups | Chests, hidden field items, one-time pickups, world-map discoveries, and treasures inside areas that later close. Is the item itself lost or only this copy? |
| Equipment and limited copies | Weapons, armor, accessories, key items, limited-quantity stock, and unique equipment. Can shops, synthesis, drops, steals, or later encounters replace it? |
| Ability sources | Equipment that teaches character abilities, Blue Magic opportunities, summons, and abilities lost when a source disappears. Is the ability still learnable elsewhere? |
| Boss steals and drops | Boss-only steals, stealable equipment, limited drops, and steal windows. Does a later source exist, and how difficult or costly is it? |
| Enemy encounters | Disc/area-exclusive enemies, variants, formation encounters, and optional bosses. Is missing the encounter relevant to the selected bestiary or achievement goals? |
| ATEs and scenes | Active Time Events, optional scenes, one-time conversations, and prerequisites affected by party location, order, or earlier choices. What exact action closes each window? |
| Sidequests and quest chains | Mognet, Friendly Creatures, Morrid's Coffees, Nero Brothers, Stellazzio, Chocobo, and other multi-stage chains. Which steps can be delayed, recovered, or permanently lost? |
| Minigames and rewards | Jump Rope, Hippaul Racing, Tetra Master events, Frog Catching, auction, and story minigames. Is the activity itself missable, or only a prize/rank/achievement? |
| Shops and synthesis | Inventory changes, limited stock, one-way shop access, synthesis recipes/materials, and price/resource constraints. Can the same item be bought or made elsewhere later? |
| Party and conditional requirements | Inventory/Gil thresholds, party composition, character-specific actions, previous choices, and conditional events. Which profile can still satisfy the condition? |
| Area access and one-way gates | Story transitions, inaccessible locations, ship/airship access changes, final-dungeon entry, and other irreversible exits. What player acknowledgement is needed before the transition? |

| Time-sensitive objectives | Excalibur II and any confirmed game-clock/event countdown. Verify timer rules, exact trigger, pause/booster effects, and build applicability independently. |
| Mutually exclusive outcomes | Branching or competing rewards and outcomes. Mark incompatible objectives so a profile does not treat both as simultaneously achievable. |

## Audit rules

- Do not label a lost chest as a lost item if another normal acquisition route exists.
- Do not describe a rare or costly later path as equivalent to an easy early pickup.
- Never mark a red warning without evidence of a permanent consequence.
- Store the latest safe action point, actual cutoff trigger, and recovery path separately.
- Surface a warning before the cutoff and in the upcoming-risk list; avoid repeating the same warning after acknowledgement unless the state changes.
