## Story outline

The map contains 44 headings transcribed as a navigation inventory from the GameFAQs walkthrough's Disc I–IV table of contents. All headings now have partial content: the nine Disc I chapters through Burmecia, sixteen Disc II chapters from Journey to Treno through Battle of the Iifa Tree, fourteen Disc III chapters through the Portal to Another World climax and Invincible escape, and five Disc IV chapters through Final Preparations and The Final Battle. Chapter IDs use explicit stable keys and are separate from displayed titles.

These are expected classes, not completion claims. The data audit will grow to validate relationships and completeness rules as verified records are added.
