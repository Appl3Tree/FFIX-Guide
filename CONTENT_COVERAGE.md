## Story outline

The map contains 44 headings transcribed as a navigation inventory from the GameFAQs walkthrough's Disc I–IV table of contents. All headings now have partial content: the nine Disc I chapters through Burmecia, sixteen Disc II chapters from Journey to Treno through Battle of the Iifa Tree, fourteen Disc III chapters through the Portal to Another World climax and Invincible escape, and five Disc IV chapters through Final Preparations and The Final Battle. Chapter IDs use explicit stable keys and are separate from displayed titles.

## Expected reference classes

- Main story, required objectives, bosses, and final encounter.
- Areas, exits, routes, shops, synthesis opportunities, treasures, hidden pickups, and world-map discoveries.
- Enemy species, variants, stats, drops, steals, abilities, encounter sets, and special mechanics.
- Equipment, items, limited copies, acquisition routes, prices, and ability-learning sources.
- Character abilities, Blue Magic, summons, and learning requirements.
- ATE triggers and prerequisites, including mutually exclusive windows.
- Sidequests, minigames, rewards, optional bosses, cards, and collection systems.
- Steam achievements, PlayStation trophies, and Xbox achievements by product/version.
- Recommended level ranges, expected difficulty, setup, equipment, party, and optional grinding notes.
- Version-specific behavior, progression gates, routes, and source provenance.

These are expected classes, not completion claims. The data audit will grow to validate relationships and completeness rules as verified records are added.
