# Research sources and method

Last updated: 2026-10-24

## Method

The project records the claim, source, relevant section, version/platform, access date, verification state, and known conflict for each fact. Walkthroughs are used for scope discovery and fact checking, not copied prose. Critical deadlines and missable consequences require corroboration. Where references disagree, the conflict stays open until evidence resolves it.

The implementation remains incomplete. All 44 chapters are partially authored and source-linked through Necron and the ending on Disc IV. The 273-source register and walkthrough do not establish that the game's content has been comprehensively researched.

## Open questions

- Alexandria's Remedy is listed by multiple walkthroughs, but its precise tile is described as the shop, a connecting passage, or an adjoining room. The opening chapter therefore says to sweep the Weapons Shop and passage.
- Alexandria's 63-Gil pickup is placed in different rooftop/chapel-side locations by guides. The route tells the player to sweep the lower side path; the exact field marker still needs direct game verification.
- Does Jump Rope's first-visit Mognet letter require a particular threshold, and is that letter available after the girls return later? Current guide copy recommends checking Mognet after both Jump Rope and the castle transition but does not classify the letter as permanently missable.
- Confirm Laudo's bookshelf interaction and Mullenkedheim's room label against an in-game route. Guides refer to the latter as both the Guest Room and East Dining Room.
- Published early Steiner steal rates and some scripted enemy data conflict. The guide lists the two item names but omits disputed odds pending further review.
- The achievement count is reconciled: 83 ATE scenes exist, the three Teach Me, Mogster! tutorials do not count, and one of the 80 eligible scenes is mutually exclusive, so the single-playthrough target is 79 of 80. GameFAQs groups Ice Cavern Lesson 2 under its Evil Forest count; the Moogle FAQ and area routes place it after freeing Mois. The individual scene index now exists, while trigger ordering and cross-event conditions remain under verification.
- Conde Petie's first visit has five achievement-eligible ATEs. GameFAQs, Jegged, and Gamer Guides give the same safe event order, but differ on compass directions for Quina Accused and Quina Can't Communicate; GameFAQs explicitly warns that entering Wendy Grocer's Item Shop before the Quina scenes closes those prompts. The event graph records the four-scene lead-in to Hungry Bryan and preserves the conflicting route descriptions.
- Plant Brain steal odds for Iron Helm vary across secondary sources. The walkthrough records the item names and later recovery path but omits a percentage.
- Level 3–4 before Plant Brain is a secondary recommendation, not a tested minimum. The standard route labels a spring-side grind optional; no Excalibur II time budget is claimed.
- The exact last safe Lindblum step for the Autograph and Moogle Suit is phrased differently across the two GameFAQs missable references. The current guide directs players to collect them after the theater event and before entering the Tantalus Hideout.
- The optional Lindblum Kingdom route starts Quina, Frog Catching, Blue Magic, and Chocobo Hot & Cold. Only the first two Chocograph treasures are included in the current tracker; remaining card availability, ability gates, rewards, and later pickup routes need a dedicated sidequest audit. The exact ATE windows that benefit from having Quina already recruited are also pending.
- Cargo Ship Black Waltz 3's weakness is disputed (none in GameFAQs versus Wind in Caves of Narshe/EliteGrindLab), as are published steal odds and their rounding. The guide records the three steal names and later Lindblum shops, without using the disputed values as route requirements.
- Lindblum sources list six local ATEs, including Teach Me, Mogster! Lesson 3; five count toward Movie Critic and the tutorial is excluded. The Festival results are mutually exclusive; Vivi's achievement mapping is documented for Steam, PlayStation, and Xbox, while mobile and Switch parity remain unresolved.
- Zaghnol appears at about 4:30 remaining in the examined Festival routes. The guide directs the player to obtain the two steals before choosing a winner; exact re-entry timing and steal rates remain unverified.
- The Ice Cavern itself remains accessible after its waterfall boss, but the Dali departure closes it. The general GameFAQs missables list and EliteGrindLab agree on the Dali cutoff; collect all eight chests before leaving the village.
- The Steam missables guide says a missed Mois letter can be recovered by returning after the boss. EliteGrindLab reports that no other publisher confirms this claim. The GameFAQs Excalibur II route visits Mois after the boss, but its route accepts the letter before the fight, so it is not independent evidence for missed-letter recovery. The guide recommends first-pass collection and labels a later return as best-effort only.
- The Ice Cavern's forced Lesson 2 ATE appears when Mois is freed. It is tracked as a tutorial scene, not as one of Movie Critic's 79 counted ATEs; Steam, PS4, and Xbox references agree that all three Mogster tutorials are excluded.
- One enemy table lists Ice as a Black Waltz 1 weakness; the other route references consistently call out Fire. The guide names Fire as the cross-source choice and retains the Ice discrepancy.
- Ice Cavern chests and boss steals are now routed, but their complete replacement paths and exact Treasure Hunter rank effects remain under item-level review.
- Dali's first-visit ATE lists conflict: GameFAQs and Gamer Guides enumerate six, while Jegged's Movie Critic checklist names five but its walkthrough also mentions Queen Brahne's Steiner. The tracker includes all six; Gamer Guides supports Cat's Eye → Cat's Eye 2 and Dagger Tries → Dagger Tries Harder and warns against the Weapon Shop until Dagger Tries Harder appears.
